Operating notes

Screen shortcuts: I opens the selected screen’s INSPECT panel; H or Space holds every display; C toggles cursors; N toggles trace snap; A/B selects a cursor; +/− zooms; [ / ] pans; F auto-fits; E enlarges; Escape restores; S saves a PNG; ? opens these notes. Use INSPECT on each bezel for Measure, View and Capture tools. Hover or focus a button for its shortcut. Shortcuts pause while editing an input.

Tap a screen to enlarge it. With cursors on, tap or drag to place A/B. Focus a screen and use arrow keys to move the selected cursor; Shift moves finely. Move over a screen for live X/Y values beside the crosshair. SNAP (N) locks to the nearest visible trace sample or FFT bin and names the trace. Left/right arrows step through samples. Turn SNAP off for free axis coordinates. Spectrograms and MIDI scores keep free coordinates because they have no trace to snap to. Matching time or frequency axes share vertical cursors. Time uses the capture clock; a cursor outside a screen’s capture stays off-screen. Zoom and pan inspect stored data; they do not add history or change audio. Auto-fit resets the selected screen’s zoom and adjusts waveform height when a waveform is selected.

SAVE PNG downloads all four displays with cursor readings, current settings and capture time. With SNAP off, frequency cursors mark axis positions; FFT resolution is sample rate ÷ 1024 (46.875 Hz at 48 kHz). Fine cursor movement does not increase measurement resolution.

Power on starts the selected input. Load an audio file or use the built-in test oscillator; microphone input starts muted. Enable LISTEN at the output connector to hear the processor. Use headphones with a microphone.

Drag a knob around its center or vertically; use its arrow keys or mouse wheel for small adjustments, Shift for finer movement, and double-click to reset. Channel scale and position affect the display. FS/div means normalized audio full scale, not calibrated electrical volts.

AUTO shows the current capture even without a trigger. NORMAL waits for a matching edge. SINGLE captures one triggered waveform and holds it; press SINGLE to re-arm. HOLD freezes the display while audio continues. Intensity, focus, graticule and persistence adjust the CRT display.

COMPARE shows input/output waveforms, spectra, spectrograms and pitch together. SPECTRUM and FORMANT include original, ideal shifted and measured spectral envelopes. MIDI SCORE shows the loaded melody and active target. CEPSTRUM and X–Y are additional views.

Pitch, vocoder, MIDI vocoder, eight-note harmony, MIDI pitch lock and dry/formant modes preserve the original viewer's controls. Pitch and harmony shifts span −12 to +24 semitones; formant shift spans −24 to +24. Bitcrush and rate reduction operate before the vocal effect. Pitch lock passes through before the first MIDI target, then holds the last target across rests. MIDI and audio restart together when seeking or looping an audio file.

Audio and MIDI stay in your browser. This instrument uses the shared Rust DSP library; it is not the Virtual Daisy ARM emulator. Fixed 1024-point FFT, 256-sample hop. The audio adapter adds roughly 21.3 ms at 48 kHz, plus browser/device latency. Harmony correction interpolates targets in pitch space. Browser decoding may resample a file to the audio context rate.
