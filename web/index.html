AN INSTRUMENT FOR THE CURIOUS.Sound in. Understanding out.

Meet your signal.

Power on, choose a source, and turn a dial. The green trace is your input; amber is the processed output. Use the test oscillator to explore without a microphone or file.

Look closer

Switch between the waveform, log-frequency spectrum, scrolling spectrogram, pitch history, and measured spectral envelope. Freeze captures the screen while audio keeps running.

Hear the change

Enable Listen to hear the output. Use headphones with the microphone. Effects bypass gives you an immediate clean reference; wet mix blends the processed signal.

Keep it light

Rust processes audio in an AudioWorklet. Eco draws at 20 fps; only the selected analysis runs. Hidden and frozen screens request no analysis. Your audio stays in this browser.

Play a melody

Load a MIDI file to drive pitch correction, the vocoder carrier, or up to eight harmony notes. MIDI and audio restart together; microphone MIDI starts at power on. Files loop independently.

This is a DSP laboratory using the shared Synthphone library, not a Virtual Daisy firmware emulator. Browser and device latency vary. Microphone capture requires HTTPS or localhost. AI made · 0rwx X
